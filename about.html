Our Story

Guangzhou Frank brings over twenty years of expertise in hydraulic applications, specializing in high-performance hydraulic solutions for excavators. Our core products include hydraulic pumps, swing motors, travel devices, reducers, and spare parts — compatible with Volvo, Doosan, Hyundai, Sany, XCMG, Liugong, SDLG and etc. These components are recognized for their excellent power stability and durability, ensuring efficient operation in extreme working conditions.

Guangzhou Frank was founded on a single principle: deliver components that match or exceed OEM specifications. Today, Frank Hydraulics supplies piston pumps, swing motors, travel motors, reduction gears, and a wide range of spare parts for excavators from 1 to 70 tons. We offer the same OEM-level quality and sourcing discipline directly to worldwide. Our commitment remains unchanged: deliver precision-engineered parts that keep your machines running.
