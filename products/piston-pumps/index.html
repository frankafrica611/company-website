Piston Pumps for Excavators

Full range of variable displacement axial piston pumps — K3V, K5V, K7V series. Designed for excavators from 5 to 50 tons.

Key Features

Swash Plate Design Variable displacement axial piston pumps with proven reliability — over 25 years of field experience in mobile applications.
Versatile Configurations Available in various rotary group layouts, tandem type with PTO, and parallel type to match your specific application.
Open Center Control Optimized for the most common hydraulic excavator systems with variable displacement pumps in open center control.
Electronic Control Ready Compatible with electronically controlled positive displacement control systems for efficient, smooth, precise operation.
