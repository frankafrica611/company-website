Swing Motors for Excavators

Compact swash plate axial piston swing motors – M5X130 and M5X180. High torque density, direct replacement for leading excavator brands.

Key Features

High Torque Density Compact design delivers exceptional torque output relative to size – ideal for space‑constrained excavator upper structures.
Compact Housing Optimized envelope dimensions allow easy integration into existing swing drive assemblies without modification.
Multiple Mounting Options Available with standard flange or spline outputs to match OEM configurations from SANY, Doosan, Volvo, and others.
Proven Reliability Field‑tested in thousands of excavators across Asia, Africa, and South America – consistent performance under heavy loads.
